Powerful Features

Built for real speed on both sides of the handover

High-efficiency tools designed specifically for restaurant managers and NGO coordinators to rescue meals without friction.

Restaurant registration

Verified profile with FSSAI licence number, address and interactive map locator.

Smart nearby matching

NGOs are ranked by real driving distance on an interactive map so food travels the shortest path.

Detailed food specifications

Categorized by cooked, raw, or packaged, veg/non-veg flag, quantities, and strict expiry timers.

One-click donation dispatch

Send to a preferred NGO or broadcast nearby. Receiving partners accept with a single tap.

Transparent live timeline

Step-by-step visual audit trail ensuring accountability from pickup to final distribution.

AI Donation Assistant AI Engine

Intelligent portion estimation, safe storage guidance, and smart distance-scoring for NGO matching.

Waste Forecasting & Analytics ML Insights
